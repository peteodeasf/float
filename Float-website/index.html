For clinicians

The tools to deliver treatment that actually works.

Float gives clinicians a structured clinical toolkit to deliver evidence-based CBT treatment for anxious kids and teens — streamlining treatment planning, between-session practice, and family communication so you can focus on what matters most: your patients.

For families

Real support for your anxious child.

When your child is struggling with anxiety, it can feel impossible to know how to help. Float connects families with clinicians who use a structured, evidence-based approach — and keeps parents actively involved in their child's recovery.
