# The parent sees the child's progress

Peter, 2026-09-10: *"we should also be able to show the parent what the teen is doing. subject to
approval by child."* Then: *"the clinician flips the permission for the child. should be able to see
the things they need to understand support: the exposure ladder, what's planned, what they've
done."*

## What the parent sees, once it's switched on

- **The ladder**: the steps in order, each with its Fear Level and whether it's done, started or not
  started yet. The same steps the child sees, and only while the child's ladder is switched on.
- **What's planned**: each exposure waiting to be done, with the day and time of day if picked.
- **What they've done**: each exposure done, with the date, and whether it went ahead or felt too
  hard.

## What the parent does not see

The child's own words and their ratings of each exposure: what they were afraid would happen, how
strongly they believed it, the Fear Level they expected and felt, what happened, and what they
learned. None of these are needed to support, and they are what a child writes knowing it goes to
their clinician.

**One thing about ratings does come through:** a step shows as Done when it has been done at least
twice and the last one ended at Fear Level 2 or under. That is the child's own rule for a finished
step, so a parent seeing "Done" can infer that last rating was low. Noted by the security review,
2026-09-10; not changed.

Exposures from the older setup that are on no step are left out. The only thing to name them by is
the text the child typed, which is not safe to show.

## Who switches it on

The clinician, on the Parent access panel, after asking the child. It records who switched it on and
when. Switching it off stops the parent seeing it straight away.

**Changes something parents see today.** The parent home already listed the child's exposures for
the next 7 days, without anyone agreeing to it. That list is "what's planned", so it now waits for the
same switch. Off is the starting state for every child.

## The child knows

When it is on, the child's Progress tab says their parent can see their ladder and what they've done.

## Checks

- `/security-review`: this changes what a parent can read about a child.
- Tests: off, the parent gets nothing about the exposures, including the old upcoming list. On, they
  get the ladder, planned and done, and none of the child's words appear anywhere in the reply. A
  parent of another family gets nothing. A clinician without access to the patient cannot switch it.
  The role-boundary test already covers a child or parent trying the clinician's switch.
