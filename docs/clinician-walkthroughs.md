## Monitoring

**What it's for:** You send the parents a monitoring form, and what they observe comes back here.
Each observation has the situation, what the child did, how the parent responded, and a Fear Level.

**Before you start:** The parents' names and emails from the patient record fill in the form for
you. An email is optional.

**Steps**
1. **Check the parents.** Each parent has a row with a label and an email. If a parent will get the
   link by text or in person, leave their email blank. [Parent 1 email]
2. **Create the form.** Click Create monitoring form. Float emails each parent who has an email.
   [Create monitoring form]
3. **Share each parent's link.** Each parent has their own link. Click Copy link and paste it into a
   text or email. [Copy link]
4. **See who has opened it.** Each parent's row says Opened or Not opened yet. It also says how many
   days ago the form was sent. [Not opened yet]
5. **Read the observations.** Under Observations, each entry shows which parent added it. If the
   parent recorded or typed a quick note, click it to read their own words. [Observations]

**What the parent sees:** An email asking them to keep a short monitoring diary for about a week,
about 5 minutes a day, with an Open monitoring form button. The link needs no login. For seven days
they get an evening email at 7pm asking "Anything come up with [child] today?". It is skipped on
days they already added something.

**Things to know**
- You can create the form only once per patient. Check both parents before you click Create
  monitoring form.
- Float does not send texts. To send the link by text, copy it yourself.
- Parents cannot edit an observation once it is saved.

## Sessions

**What it's for:** This patient's session notes. Type a note after a session, or record the session
on your phone and get a draft note with a transcript.

**Before you start:** Nothing, to type a note. To record, a phone browser that can record, such as
Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android.

**Steps**
1. **Type a note.** Click + Add note. Choose who the session was with, add tags, set the date, write
   the note and click Save. [+ Add note]
2. **Record a session.** Click Record session. The first time, record who agreed to the recording and
   click Continue. Then choose who is in the session and click Start recording. [Record session]
3. **Stop the recording.** Use Pause and Resume as needed. When the session ends, click Stop and
   save. [Stop and save]
4. **Check the draft.** While Float writes up the session, a Recording row shows here. The note then
   appears marked "Draft · from a recording". Click Transcript to read it. Click a speaker's name to
   change who they are. [Transcript]
5. **Approve the draft.** Click Approve note. Or click …, then Edit, change the note, and click
   Update and approve. [Approve note]

**What the family sees:** Nothing. Notes, recordings and transcripts are only in the clinician app.

**Things to know**
- Agreement to record is asked once per patient. Later recordings do not ask again.
- Float deletes the audio once the transcript is saved. The transcript is kept with the note.
- An approved note is added to the Case Summary. A draft is not, until you approve it.

## Plan: Ladder

**What it's for:** The child's steps toward the situations they avoid, easiest first, each with a
Fear Level. Exposures are set up from here.

**Before you start:** Situations from monitoring. You can write rungs before you meet the child.

**Steps**
1. **Build the ladder.** Edit ladder opens a conversation that works through the rungs with the
   child. ⛶ Full screen opens the same conversation as session mode. [Edit ladder]
2. **Check each Fear Level.** 1 to 10. Click the number to change it. The ladder sorts easiest
   first. [Fear Level]
3. **Do the downward arrow on a rung.** Do it with the child. The button shows a tick when it's
   done. [Downward Arrow]
4. **Set up an exposure.** Set it up opens full screen so the child can see it. The child answers,
   you type. You can leave the day for the child to pick at home. [Set it up]
5. **Make the ladder visible to the child.** The switch puts the whole ladder in the child's app. It
   needs at least one rung. [Visible to patient]

**What the child sees:** Nothing until the switch is on. An exposure you set up shows in the
child's app, waiting for a day if you left it open.

**Things to know**
- A downward arrow is saved as yours, even when the child did it with you.
- The situation buttons only change what you see. The switch and Edit ladder still apply to the
  whole ladder.

## Plan: Accommodations

**What it's for:** What the parents do to ease the child's anxiety, under each situation, easiest
to stop first. Each has a Fear Level for how hard it would be for the child if the parent stopped.
You also set where the parent is with stopping each one.

**Before you start:** A treatment plan, made on the Ladder tab. The situations on the ladder appear
here.

**Steps**
1. **Open the builder.** Build plan shows each situation as a card, where you add, edit or remove
   accommodations. [Build plan]
2. **Add an accommodation.** Type what the parent does, enter a Fear Level and click Add. [Add]
3. **Add from monitoring.** Under "From monitoring — tap to add", tap a suggestion to add it. Some
   show the parent's own estimate. [From monitoring — tap to add]
4. **Rate with the child.** Child ratings opens a full-screen sheet. Ask the child how hard it would
   be if the parent stopped each one, and type what they say. [Child ratings]
5. **Save.** Save plan closes the builder. [Save plan]
6. **Set the status.** Plan it lets you choose Not started, Working on it or Stopped. [Plan it]

**What the parent sees:** Each accommodation marked Working on it gets a weekly check-in: "This
week, did you hold the line?", with Every time, Mostly and I gave in. On the parent's home screen,
accommodations show under the child's upcoming exposures, and the parent can write "How did it go?"
notes. The latest note shows on your list as "Parent: …".

**Things to know**
- Only you set the status. The parent cannot change it.
- The child never sees the parent's estimate. You see it as ≈ next to the Fear Level.
- A Fear Level you type in Build plan counts as your guess. One entered on the Child ratings sheet
  counts as the child's rating.

## Progress

**What it's for:** How the child's exposures are going, and the parent's weekly check-ins.

**Before you start:** At least one completed exposure, for the graph and averages.

**Steps**
1. **Read the numbers across the top.** Total exposures, this week's completed, longest streak, and
   the average change in belief and Fear Level. [Exposures]
2. **Switch the graph.** Progress over time has one line per situation. Choose Belief in Prediction
   or Fear Level. [Fear Level]
3. **Pick a week and a day.** Use the arrows to change week. Click a day to see its exposures, each
   with Predicted and Actual. [Previous week]
4. **Check the parent's work.** Under Parent, Weekly check-ins shows each answer. [Weekly check-ins]

**What the family sees:** This tab is only in the clinician app. The parent sees the child's ladder,
what is planned and what is done, only if you tick "Parents can see the child's progress" under
Access → Parent. The parent never sees the child's ratings or what the child writes.

**Things to know**
- A missed exposure has an amber dot. Green is done, red is too hard, and teal is scheduled.
- Exposures set up in session and ones the child set up in their app both count.

## Chat

**What it's for:** Messages between sessions with the child and with each parent. Each person has
their own thread with you.

**Before you start:** Invite the child and each parent under Access first.

**Steps**
1. **Pick a thread.** The child is first, then each parent by email. [Teen · private thread]
2. **Check who you are writing to.** A parent's thread shows "THIS PARENT ONLY". [THIS PARENT ONLY]
3. **Write and send.** Type a message and click Send, or press Enter. [Send]

**What the family sees:** The child replies in the child app, and each parent in the parent app.

**Things to know**
- The parent never sees the child's messages. The child never sees a parent's. Two parents do not
  see each other's threads.
- No email or text goes out when you send a message. The family sees it when they open the app.
- The number on the Chat tab counts only the child's unread messages.

## Access (Teen, Parent, Clinician)

**What it's for:** Who can sign in for this patient. Teen and Parent invite the family to their
apps. Clinician sets which clinicians at your practice can open the record.

**Before you start:** The parent's permission to invite the child. An email for each person.

**Steps**
1. **Invite the child.** Click Teen. If it says "Awaiting parent consent", click "Record consent
   (obtained offline)" once you have it. Enter the child's email and click Send invite. [Teen]
2. **Invite a parent.** Click Parent, enter an email under "Invite another parent" and click Invite
   parent. Repeat for a second parent. [Invite parent]
3. **Check the parents.** Each parent shows "Has signed in" or "Has not signed in yet". Remove takes
   a parent off this child. [Remove]
4. **Choose what parents can see.** Tick "Parents can see the child's progress". The child's ratings
   of the accommodations are a separate tick. [Parents can see the child's progress]
5. **Manage clinicians.** Click Clinician to see who can open this patient, and to add or remove a
   colleague. [Clinician]

**What the family sees:** An invitation email with a login link and a temporary password. The child
sets their own password on first sign-in. A parent who already uses Float for another child keeps
their password.

**Things to know**
- Resend invite gives the child a new temporary password. The old one stops working.
- The … menu has Edit profile, Move to maintenance and Close treatment. Close treatment turns off
  the child's app and the parent's app.
