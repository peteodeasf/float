# Separation anxiety: triggers and possible rungs

**From Dr. Walker, 2026-08-31.** Her words, transcribed as given.

This replaces the case set we built ourselves, which she judged a bad place to start. **Partial —
from "Parents go to a party" onwards these are situations with no rungs yet.**

---

## At home, parent in a different room from the child (child cannot see or hear the parent)

- Be in a different room from the child for: 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, etc.

## Be in a room alone

- Child is alone in a room, cannot hear or see where the parent is: 30 seconds, 1 minute,
  2 minutes, 5 minutes, etc.

## Sleep in own room without sibling, pet or parent

- Get ready for bed, lie in room by self in bed: 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, etc.
- Increase amount of time on a planned course
- All safety behaviours (special good nights, check-ins, special lights, etc.) need to be stopped
  before doing these exposures

## School drop off

- Imagine being dropped off at school, saying your goodbyes, walking by yourself to your classroom,
  feeling anxiety and fear and still doing it — play it repeatedly in your head like a movie made
  for you
- Watch videos of kids getting dropped off at school
- Practice "drop offs" repeatedly on non-school days, after school. Make it feel real, including
  the parent driving away for a short period of time
- Send the child in a carpool
- Allow the father to drive if the mother is the preferred safety provider
- Make sure the child can be apart from the parent at home first

## First day of school

- Watch videos or movies about the first day of school — not ones that attempt to provide comfort,
  just ones showing kids having a first day of school
- Imagine — make your own movie of — your first day at school: saying goodbye, not knowing what to
  expect, who will be there, what it will be like, not knowing where your parents are or whether
  they are safe
- Practice the first day of school: role play, enact with parents or siblings or another adult
  friend acting as teacher, principal, etc. Do this repeatedly

## New teacher

*(no rungs given)*

## Parents go out for dinner with a sitter present

- Imagine — make your own movie of — your parents telling you they are going out to dinner. They
  don't tell you where, how far away it is, or exactly when they will be back, and you are not
  allowed to call or text them, or ask your sitter to do so or to reassure you. That they are away
  and there is no way to know what's happening until they return.
- Parents leave the house for 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, etc.
- Parents go for a short walk

## Situations with no rungs yet

- Parents go to a party
- Parents travel
- New sitter
- New school
- Parent late for pick up
- Sleep over at friend's house
- Go to summer camp
- Go to sleep away summer camp
- Attend school
- Attend sport games, lessons or training
- Go to college
