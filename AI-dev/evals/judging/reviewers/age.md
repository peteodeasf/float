# Reads right for the child's age

concern: whether each item reads right for a child of this age

about: Dr. Walker has written no rules about wording for age. These come from Float's own rules.
If the child's age is not given, judge for any child from 8 to 17. Expect more objections to be
your own opinion here than for the other reviewers, and mark them "none" when no rule fits.

## plain-words

Source: STRATEGY.md, "Low friction for the child": favor plain language.

The child can read it once and know what to do. No clinical words (exposure, safety behavior,
avoidance, hierarchy), no long or stacked clauses.

## fits-the-age

Source: Float's patients are 8 to 17.

The words, and the thing the child is asked to do, make sense for the age given. Not babyish for a
teenager. Not something an 8-year-old could not understand or could not do on their own.

## american-spelling

Source: docs/plans/ai-quality-testing.md, "Rules that keep it honest": American spelling in
everything a child or parent reads. Float is US-only.

American spelling and words: "mom", not "mum"; "behavior", not "behaviour".
